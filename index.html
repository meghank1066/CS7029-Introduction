02 STAGE 2

[ COMPUTER GRAPHICS ]

Computer Graphics.

02

An area which cites and utilizes computer graphics that I find particularly interesting would be in virtual reality and headsets, I find this interesting due to a technique I learnt known as barrel distortion correction which is essentially a technique designed to trick the human brain which involves consciously distorting and warping the images in a scene, this is done in order to facilitate the graphics for the thick lens that virtual reality headsets have. Together they facilitate the human brain by creating the illusion of immersion which is what makes virtual reality special.

I believe Computer graphics in today's world is excellent at providing infinite random worlds in real time for video games and virtual reality experiences which in official terms is known as procedural generation. Instead of building everything by hand, it utilises mathematics and code to generate complex worlds instantly based on what the user does. Traditional art like paintings or sculptures can be limited as an artist can only create so much by hand. Computer graphics has the ability to use computer power to calculate millions of details at once unlike traditional forms of media, which couldn't dynamically calculate and show things such as real-time wind blowing through grass or weather changing it's landscape as you interact with it.

03 STAGE 3

[ END-OF-COURSE GOAL ]

What I would like to create.

By the end of this course, I would love to create an interactive 3D interpretation of The Secret Garden by Frances Hodgson Burnett, which is a favourite novel of mine. I would like to bring the garden to life as an immersive environment, turning the descriptions from the novel into a visual and interactive space. I imagine an overgrown Victorian garden with climbing ivy, roses, trees, a secret doorway and a pond. I would like the garden to feel as though it has been abandoned for years but is gradually coming alive again as the user explores it.


I am particularly interested in using 3D modelling, textures and lighting, animation as well as environmental effects such as rain and wind to create this moody but alive atmosphere. Rather than simply recreating the garden from the novel, I would like to create my own interpretation of it and design an experience that captures the feeling of discovery and transformation in The Secret Garden. I like the idea of using computer graphics to bring a setting from a book that I love into a space that the user can actually explore.
